Week 7: LED Strategy for Mushroom Growth

Developed a stage-by-stage LED lighting strategy and designed a 3-chamber experiment to test how light colour and photoperiod affect oyster mushroom yield, size, and quality.

Completed
May 2026 Week 7 of 14 Experiment Design EEE · University of Peradeniya

Key Findings from Literature

Light colour and duration do not significantly affect total yield or growth speed, but are crucial for improving physical size and quality.
Blue light (~475nm) promotes the largest caps, thickest stems, and highest fresh weight in the fruiting stage.
White light (W:12h) produces the heaviest mushrooms on average — a strong alternative to blue for yield-focused farming.
Green light accelerates the reproductive stage and shortens the time to pinhead formation.
Red light promotes early, strong mycelium growth during the vegetative stage but must be switched off after full colonisation.

Data Collection Metrics

The following variables will be logged for each chamber to produce solid engineering results:

If Chamber B yields the most mass but Chamber C yields the largest individual mushrooms, the microcontroller will be programmed with selectable presets — Mode 1: Max Yield and Mode 2: Max Size.
